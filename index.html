Our Story

The journey of Odallo Guitars and the man behind every instrument — Vincent Odallo.

Vincent Odallo

In the heart of Nairobi, Kenya, Vincent Odallo turns passion into precision. As the founder and master luthier of Odallo Guitars, he handcrafts every instrument from scratch using premium tonewoods, expert fretwork, and high-quality electronics.

Vincent’s journey began with a deep love for music and a desire to create instruments that truly connect with the player. Each guitar is a collaboration between artist and craftsman — built to your exact vision, from neck profile to finish.

Beyond building new instruments, Vincent offers professional repairs, restoration, and upgrades, breathing new life into beloved guitars with the same care and attention to detail.
